**原文**

> As these and endless other stories about the late 1980s suggest, the system’s collapse had been profoundly unexpected and unimaginable to many Soviet people until it happened, and yet, it quickly appeared perfectly logical and exciting when it began. **原文**

> One of the central contradictions of socialism is a version of what Claude Lefort called a general paradox within the ideology of modernity: the split between ideological enunciation (which reflects the theoretical ideals of the Enlightenment) and ideological rule (manifest in the practical concerns of the modern state’s political authority). The paradox, that we will call “Lefort’s paradox,” lies in the fact that ideological rule must be “abstracted from any question concerning its origins,” thus remaining outside of ideological enunciation and, as a result, rendering that enunciation deficient. In other words, to fulfill its political function of reproducing power, the ideological discourse must claim to represent an “objective truth” that exists outside of it; however, the external nature of this “objective truth” renders the ideological discourse inherently lacking in the means to describe it in total, which can ultimately undermine this discourse’s legitimacy and the power that it supports. This inherent contradiction of any version of modern ideology, argues Lefort, can be concealed only by the figure of the “master,” who, by being presented as standing outside ideological discourse and possessing external knowledge of the objective truth, temporarily conceals the contradiction by allowing it “to appear through himself ” (1986, 211–12).20 In other words, modern ideological discourse, based on the utopian ideals of the Enlightenment, gains its legitimacy from an imaginary position that is external to it and will experience a crisis of legitimacy if that imaginary external position is questioned or destroyed.

**译文**

> 社会主义的核心矛盾之一，是克洛德·勒福尔所说的现代性意识形态内部一个普遍悖论的变体：即意识形态的“陈述”（enunciation，反映启蒙运动的理论理想）与意识形态的“统治”（rule，体现于现代国家政治权威的实际关切）之间的分裂。我们称之为“勒福尔的悖论”的这一悖论，在于意识形态的统治必须“抽离于任何关于其起源的问题”，从而外在于意识形态的陈述，并因此使该陈述有所欠缺。换言之，为了履行其再生产权力的政治功能，意识形态话语必须声称代表一种存在于它之外的“客观真理”；然而，这种“客观真理”的外在性使意识形态话语在手段上内在地缺乏将其整体描述的能力，这最终可能削弱这一话语的合法性及其所支撑的权力。勒福尔认为，任何版本的现代意识形态所固有的这一矛盾，只能靠“主人”这一形象来掩盖：他被呈现为立于意识形态话语之外、拥有关于客观真理的外在知识，从而让这一矛盾“经由他自身显现出来”，将其暂时遮蔽（1986, 211–12）。20换言之，以启蒙运动的乌托邦理想为基础的现代意识形态话语，其合法性来自一个外在于它的想象性位置，一旦这个想象性的外在位置受到质疑或被摧毁，它就会遭遇合法性危机。

**原文**

> In the society built on communist ideals, this paradox appeared through the announced objective of achieving the full liberation of the society and individual (building of communism, creation of the New Man) by means of subsuming that society and individual under full party control. The Soviet citizen was called upon to submit completely to party leadership, to cultivate a collectivist ethic, and repress individualism, while at the same time becoming an enlightened and independentminded individual who pursues knowledge and is inquisitive and creative.21 This Soviet version of Lefort’s paradox was not a chance development; it grew out of the very revolutionary project itself. In 1825, Saint-Simon, an early theorist of the political, intellectual, and artistic avant-garde, whose ideas influenced Marx, Lenin, and Russian revolutionaries, wrote that the project of liberating the society required establishing a political and aesthetic avant-garde that would exercise “over society a positive power, a true priestly function . . . marching forcefully in the van of all the intellectual faculties.” This avantgarde, wrote Saint-Simon, should address itself “to the imagination and to the sentiments of mankind [and] should therefore always exercise the liveliest and most decisive action.” For this purpose the arts and politics should unite under “a common drive and a general idea” (quoted in Egbert 1967, 343).

**译文**

> 在建立于共产主义理想之上的社会中，这一悖论通过如下宣告的目标显现出来：通过将社会和个人完全纳入党的控制之下，实现社会与个人的彻底解放（建设共产主义、造就新人［New Man］）。苏联公民被要求完全服从党的领导、培养集体主义伦理并压抑个人主义，与此同时又要成为一个开明而有独立思想的人——追求知识、好奇而富有创造性。21这种苏联版的勒福尔悖论并非偶然的发展；它恰恰源于革命方案本身。1825年，政治、知识和艺术先锋派的早期理论家圣西门——其思想影响了马克思、列宁和俄国革命者——写道，解放社会的方案要求建立一个政治和美学的先锋派，它将对“社会行使一种积极的权力，一种真正的祭司职能……强有力地行进在所有智识能力的前列”。圣西门写道，这一先锋派应当诉诸“人类的想象与情感，因此应当始终发挥最活跃、最决定性的作用”。为此，艺术与政治应当在“共同的驱动力和普遍的理念”之下联合起来（转引自 Egbert 1967, 343）。

### Segment 5

**原文**

> The conception of a political and artistic avant-garde as a creative force united by one idea for the purposes of leading and perfecting society put this tandem before an enduring paradox: the process of leading and perfecting had to be subsumed under the control of a political program and, at the same time, to be free from control in order to focus on the creative, experimental, and innovating process for the production of a beter future (Egbert 1967, 343–46).

**译文**

### Segment 6

**原文**

> In the Russian revolutionary context, this paradox of modern ideology became institutionalized by the Bolshevik Revolution of 1917. The new process of cultural production was supposed to advance radical social ideas and revolutionize consciousness by achieving two relatively incommensurable goals: to practice an experimental, innovative aesthetics that was constantly ready to defy old canons and, at the same time, to subsume these creative experimentations and innovations under the strict control of the vanguard party. Immediately after the revolution, Lenin wrote in a letter to Clara Zetkin that Communists could not sit in idleness allowing the “cultural process” to develop chaotically: they “must strive with clear consciousness to control that entire process in order to form and define its results” (Arnol’dov et al. 1984, 176). Lenin accused members of the Second International of separatism because some of them argued that, having come to power, the proletariat should stop interfering with creative cultural production and experimentation. On the contrary, argued Lenin, the only means of achieving the goal of the ultimate liberation of culture and consciousness in communism was to intensify the party’s management of all spheres of cultural life. A person could not become truly liberated spontaneously; that person had to be educated and cultivated. On Lenin’s insistence, the Bolshevik Party adopted a resolution stressing that all organizations of the Proletkul’t (People’s Commissariat of Pro letarian Culture) had “an unconditional obligation to regard themselves as strictly subsidiary organs” to the organizations of the Narkompros (People’s Commissariat of Enlightenment) (Arnol’dov et al. 1984, 171). In other words, cultural organizations (all forms of intellectual, scientific, and artistic practice) were subsidiary to educational and political organizations, and all forms of cultural production were to be fully supervised by the party. It was that subsidiary position, went the argument, that would allow these organizations to exercise their full creative potential for the building of the new society.

**译文**

> 在俄国革命的语境中，现代意识形态的这一悖论因1917年布尔什维克革命而得以制度化。新的文化生产过程本应通过达成两个相对不可通约的目标，来推进激进的社会理念并革新意识：一方面践行一种实验性、创新性的美学，随时准备挑战旧有的规范；另一方面，又要将这些创造性的实验与创新纳入先锋党的严格控制之下。革命后不久，列宁在致克拉拉·蔡特金的信中写道，共产党人不能袖手旁观，任由“文化过程”混乱地发展：他们“必须以清醒的意识努力控制整个过程，以形成并界定其结果”（Arnol'dov 等 1984, 176）。列宁指责第二国际的成员搞分离主义，因为其中一些人主张，无产阶级一旦掌权就应当停止干预创造性的文化生产与实验。列宁论证说，恰恰相反，在共产主义中实现文化与意识最终解放的唯一途径，就是加强党对文化生活各个领域的管控。一个人不可能自发地获得真正的解放；他必须被教育和培养。在列宁的坚持下，布尔什维克党通过了一项决议，强调无产阶级文化协会（Proletkul't，无产阶级文化人民委员部）的所有组织都“有绝对义务把自己视为”教育人民委员部（Narkompros）各组织的“严格附属机关”（Arnol'dov 等 1984, 171）。换言之，文化组织（一切形式的知识、科学与艺术实践）从属于教育与政治组织，一切形式的文化生产都要受到党的全面监督。这一论证认为，正是这种附属地位，才能使这些组织为建设新社会充分发挥其创造潜力。

### Segment 7

**原文**

> The Soviet state’s constant anxiety about publicly justifying state control of cultural production while simultaneously attempting to promote its independence and experimentation reflected this paradox. As late as 1984, a book entitled Marxist-Leninist Theory of Culture (Marksistsko-leninskaia teoriia kul’tury), authored by a collective of theoreticians of culture from Moscow’s Institute of Marxism-Leninism (Arnol’dov et al.), was still defending this point. Some may say—their book begins—that to be truly creative, the work of cultural production in intellectual, scientific, and artistic fields cannot be controlled and directed. The book goes on to argue that although this view is not altogether erroneous, it tells only one side of the story, ignoring the irreducible duality of all cultural production. In fact, the book argues, creative work is always both “a strictly private affair” of a creative individual and a “labor of social utility” that creates “spiritual values” and “socio-moral norms” in society. In the socialist society, both aspects of cultural production are recognized as equally important, since in this society “the formation of the new person goes not spontaneously, but consciously, as a result of a purposeful educational work.” Therefore, in the socialist context, the independence of creativity and the control of creative work by the party are not mutually contradictory but must be pursued simultaneously (Arnol’dov et al. 1984, 162, 163). What is remarkable about the discourse in this book is not the argument itself but that this imaginary dispute needed to be revisited throughout Soviet history, suggesting the enduring tension at socialism’s core.

**译文**

> 苏联国家一方面要公开为党对文化生产的控制辩护，另一方面又要促进其独立性与实验性，这种持续的焦虑正反映了这一悖论。直到1984年，一本由莫斯科马克思列宁主义研究院的文化理论家集体撰写的《马克思列宁主义文化理论》（Marksistsko-leninskaia teoriia kul'tury，Arnol'dov 等）仍在为这一论点辩护。该书开篇写道：或许有人说，要在知识、科学和艺术领域真正富有创造性地从事文化生产，就不可能被控制和指挥。该书接着论证说，这种观点虽不无道理，却只讲了问题的一面，忽略了所有文化生产不可化约的双重性。事实上，该书认为，创造性工作既始终是创造性个体“纯属私人的事务”，又是一种创造社会“精神价值”和“社会道德规范”的“社会公益劳动”。在社会主义社会中，文化生产的这两个方面被承认同等重要，因为在这个社会中，“新人的形成不是自发地，而是自觉地进行的，是有目的的教育工作的结果”。因此，在社会主义语境下，创造性的独立与党对创造性工作的控制并不相互矛盾，而必须同时推进（Arnol'dov 等 1984, 162, 163）。这本书中话语的引人注目之处不在于论点本身，而在于这一想象性的争论在整个苏联历史上需要被反复重提，这昭示了社会主义核心处持久的张力。

### Segment 8

**原文**

> This tension was not limited to scientific and artistic spheres but concerned all discourses and forms of knowledge that were produced and circulated in Soviet society. In the earlier periods of Soviet history, as the following chapters will show, the loud voices of the political, scientific, and artistic avant-garde concealed this paradox. They located themselves “outside” the field of ideological discourse and from that external position made public comments about and adjustments to that discourse. An explosion of creativity and experimentation marked the early postrevolutionary years but ultimately gave way to the suppression of the intellectual avant-garde and all experimental culture and science and to the introduction of a strict and unified party control.22 This shift was enabled and made to appear logical by the paradox inherent in the very ideology of the revolutionary project.

**译文**

> 这种张力并不局限于科学与艺术领域，而是涉及苏联社会中生产和流通的一切话语与知识形式。正如随后各章将显示的，在苏联历史的较早时期，政治、科学和艺术先锋派的高亢声音掩盖了这一悖论。他们将自己置于意识形态话语领域“之外”，从那个外在位置对这一话语公开发表评论并加以调整。革命后最初几年以创造和实验的爆发为标志，但最终让位于对知识先锋派及一切实验性文化与科学的压制，以及严格统一的党的控制的引入。22使这一转变成为可能并显得合乎逻辑的，正是革命方案意识形态本身所固有的悖论。

### Segment 9

**原文**

> It was Stalin who now played the role of Lefort’s “master” who stood outside of ideological discourse, making editorial comments about it from that external position and in this way concealing the paradox through himself. This external position enabled the production and wide circulation of a public metadiscourse about all forms of political, artistic, and scientific expression that evaluated them for precision and accuracy against an external canon—the Marxist-Leninist dogma. Stalin’s “external” editorial position vis-à-vis all forms of discourse and knowledge, which provided him with unique access to the external canon against which to evaluate them, was crucial in the emergence of those phenomena that became the trademarks of his regime: his immense political power; the cult of his personality; his personal involvement in editing political speeches, scientific papers, films, and musical compositions; the campaign of purges in party organizations; and the ultimate Great Terror in which millions perished. In the last years of Stalin’s rule, and especially after his death in 1953 and the subsequent denunciation of his cult of personality, that external position vis-à-vis discourse and knowledge vanished. The main result of this development was not the denunciation of a concrete leader, but a major reorganization of the entire discursive regime of state socialism: a position external to ideological (political, scientific, artistic) discourse, from which a metadiscourse about it could be launched, ceased to exist, and therefore the metadiscourse on ideology disappeared from public circulation (see discussion of this process in chapter 2).

**译文**

> 正是斯大林扮演了勒福尔所说的“主人”的角色：他立于意识形态话语之外，从那个外在位置对之加以编辑性的评论，并以这种方式通过自身掩盖了悖论。这一外在位置使一种关于一切政治、艺术和科学表达的公共元话语得以生产和广泛流传，这种元话语依据一个外在的规范——马克思列宁主义教条——来评判这些表达的精确性与准确性。斯大林相对于一切话语和知识形式的“外在”编辑位置，使他得以独享那个用以评判它们的外在规范，这一位置对造就其政权种种标志性现象至关重要：他巨大的政治权力；他的个人崇拜；他亲自参与修改政治演说、科学论文、电影和音乐作品；党组织中的清洗运动；以及最终使数百万人丧生的大恐怖。在斯大林统治的最后几年，尤其在他1953年去世以及随后对其个人崇拜的谴责之后，相对于话语和知识的那个外在位置消失了。这一发展的主要结果不是对某个具体领导人的谴责，而是对整个国家社会主义话语体制的重大重组：一个外在于意识形态（政治的、科学的、艺术的）话语、可以由此发起关于它的元话语的位置不复存在，因此关于意识形态的元话语从公共流通中消失了（参见第二章对这一过程的讨论）。

### Segment 10

**原文**

> Since there was no longer an external voice that could conceal the Lefort’s paradox of Soviet ideology, the incongruence of goals and means that constituted that paradox became unleashed. This change ultimately led to a profound transformation of the structure of all types of Soviet ideological discourse (from the language of ideology to the nature of ideological rituals, practices, and organizations) during late socialism. As a result of that transformation, it became less important to read ideological representations for “literal” (referential) meanings than to reproduce their precise structural forms. This transformation of the discursive regime eventually led to a profound shift within Soviet culture during the late period, opening up spaces of indeterminacy, creativity, and unanticipated meanings in the context of strictly formulaic ideological forms, rituals, and organizations. In this way Lefort’s paradox returned to haunt the Soviet system. It enabled a profound internal reinterpretation and displacement of the socialist system, creating a set of contradictory conditions that made the system’s implosion seem so unexpected when it began, and at the same time so unsurprising and fast once it had occurred.

**译文**

> 由于不再有能够掩盖苏联意识形态之勒福尔悖论的外在声音，构成该悖论的目标与手段之间的不一致便得以释放。这一变化最终导致晚期社会主义时期苏联各种意识形态话语结构（从意识形态的语言到意识形态仪式、实践和组织的性质）的深刻转型。由于这一转型，解读意识形态表征的“字面”（指涉性）意义变得不如复现其精确的结构形式重要。话语体制的这一转型最终导致苏联晚期文化内部的深刻转变，在严格程式化的意识形态形式、仪式和组织的语境中，打开了不确定性、创造性和未曾预料的意义的空间。就这样，勒福尔的悖论回来纠缠苏联体制。它使社会主义体制得以发生深刻的内部重新诠释与位移，制造出一系列相互矛盾的条件，使得当体制开始崩塌时显得如此出人意料，而一旦崩塌发生，又显得如此毫不意外、如此迅速。

### Segment 3

**原文**

> This standardization of the form of discourse developed gradually, as a result of the disappearance, in the 1950s, of the external editorial voice that commented on that discourse. With that shift, the form of the ideological representations became fixed and replicated—unchanged from one context to the next. These representations no longer had to be read literally, at least in most contexts, to work perfectly well as elements of the hegemonic representation. This fixed and normalized discursive system was akin to the kind of discourse that Bakhtin terms “authoritative discourse” (avtoritetnoe slovo). For Bakhtin, authoritative discourse coheres around a strict external idea or dogma (whether religious, political, or otherwise) and occupies a particular position within the discursive regime of a period. It has two main features. First, because of a special “script” in which it is coded, authoritative discourse is sharply demarcated from all other types of discourse that coexist with it, which means that it does not depend on them, it precedes them, and it cannot be changed by them. Second, all these other types of discourse are organized around it. Their existence depends on being positioned in relation to it, having to refer to it, quote it, praise it, interpret it, apply it, and so forth, but they cannot, for example, interfere with its code and change it. Regardless of whether this demarcated and fixed authoritative discourse is successful in persuading its authors and audiences, they experience it as immutable and therefore unquestionable (Bakhtin 1994, 342–43).23 To stress that during late socialism the newly normalized Soviet ideological discourse no longer functioned at the level of meaning as a kind of ideology in the usual sense of the word, I will refer to it henceforth as “authoritative discourse.”

**译文**

> 这种话语形式的标准化是逐步形成的，其结果是1950年代那个对这一话语加以评说的外在编辑声音的消失。随着这一转变，意识形态表征的形式变得固定并被不断复制——从一种语境到另一种语境毫无变化。这些表征不再需要被按字面去阅读（至少在大多数语境中如此），便可作为霸权表征的要素而运作得十分良好。这个固定而规范化的话语体系，近似于巴赫金所说的那种“权威话语”（avtoritetnoe slovo）。在巴赫金看来，权威话语围绕某一严格外在的观念或教条（无论是宗教的、政治的或其他性质的）凝聚而成，并在一个时期的话语体制中占据特定的位置。它具有两个主要特征。第一，由于权威话语以某种特殊的“脚本”编码，它被与同它共存的一切其他话语类型截然区分开来，这意味着它不依赖于它们，先于它们存在，且不能为它们所改变。第二，所有这些其他类型的话语都围绕它组织起来。它们的存在取决于如何被置于与它的关系之中：它们必须参照它、引用它、赞美它、阐释它、运用它，等等，但例如它们不能干预它的代码并改变它。无论这种被划界且固定化的权威话语能否成功地说服它的作者与受众，他们都把它体验为不可改变的，因而是不容置疑的（巴赫金 1994，342–43）。23为了强调在晚期社会主义时期，新近规范化的苏联意识形态话语在意义层面上已不再作为通常意义上的意识形态而运作，我在此后将其称为“权威话语”。

> 世代并非天然之物，而是通过共同经验以及关于这种经验的话语被生产出来的。在适当的条件下，年龄可以提供卡尔·曼海姆所说的“社会过程之历史维度中的共同位置”，从而形成看待这一过程的共同视角（Mannheim 1952, 290）。在特定时期长大成人的共同经验，也可能有助于形成共同的理解与意义，以及这些理解与意义得以再生产的诸过程（Rofel 1999, 22）。德马蒂尼（DeMartini, 1985）强调对世代的两种不同理解：作为队列和作为谱系。队列理解强调年龄的差异，假定同龄人彼此之间有某些共同之处，也具有将他们与其他队列区分开来的特征。谱系理解强调世代之间的关系，假定父母与子女之间有着牢固的纽带，以及社会和政治意识的连续性。这两种对世代的理解决不必然互相矛盾。它们可以共存，本书正是在这种意义上理解世代的。在俄罗斯，关于世代经验之重要性的论述广泛而有力。本书中出现的许多人都思考和谈论自己在苏联晚期成长经历的重要性。在俄罗斯，人们普遍比较不同世代的经验，用特定的名称来指认他们，提及那些被视为对形成共同世代经验具有重要意义的事件和文化现象，描述世代之间的连续性，如此等等。这些话语不仅反映世代，也参与世代的生成。

### Segment 8

**原文**

> This study focuses on this younger generation—people who were born between the 1950s and early 1970s and came of age between the 1970s and the mid-1980s (see also Boym 1994; Lur’e 1997 and 1998). In the mid-1980s approximately 90 million people, almost one-third of the Soviet population, were between the ages of 15 and 34—therefore belonging to what I am calling the last Soviet generation.46 Although diferences in social class, gender, education, ethnicity, profession, geographic area, and language provided for diferences in the experiences of socialism by these people, they nevertheless shared particular understandings, meanings, and processes of that period, having come of age during the 1970s and mid-1980s. As Russian philologist Marina Kniazeva has pointed out, that generation of people, whom she calls “the children of stagnation” (deti zastoia), unlike previous and subsequent generations, had no “inaugural event” around which to coalesce as a cohort (1990). The identity of the older generations was formed around events such as the revolution, the war, the denunciation of Stalin; the identity of the younger generations has been formed around the collapse of the Soviet Union. Unlike these older and younger groups, the common identity of the last Soviet generation was formed by a shared experience of the normalized, ubiquitous, and immutable authoritative discourse of Brezhnev’s years.

**译文**

> 本书主张，由于权威话语的述行性转向以及该话语随后的规范化，斯大林之后的时期——1950年代中期至1980年代中期——开始被视为一个具有共同特征的特定时期，本书称之为晚期社会主义。在涉及这一时期的某些文献中，这三十年被划分为上文提及的两个较短的时期：解冻（otepel’，即赫鲁晓夫改革的时期）和停滞（zastoi，即勃列日涅夫的时期）。1968年夏天苏联对捷克斯洛伐克的干预，通常被视为两者之间象征性的分界（Strada 1998, 11）。这两个时期大致对应两代人——较年长的一代有时被称为“六十年代人”（shestidesiatniki，以其成长年代命名），以及较年轻的一群，即本书所称的“最后的苏联世代”。

### Segment 9

**原文**

> Most people of that generation were also members of the Komsomol during the 1970s and 1980s. This complex relationship, as argued earlier, allowed them to maintain an afinity for the many aesthetic possibilities and ethical values of socialism, while at the same time interpreting them in new terms that were not necessarily anticipated by the state—thus avoiding many of the system’s limitations and forms of controls.

**译文**

> 本研究聚焦于这较年轻的一代——出生于1950年代至1970年代初、在1970年代至1980年代中期长大成人的那些人（另见Boym 1994；Lur’e 1997和1998）。1980年代中期，大约有九千万人——几乎占苏联人口的三分之一——年龄在15岁至34岁之间，因而属于我所称的最后的苏联世代。46 尽管社会阶级、性别、教育、族裔、职业、地理区域和语言的差异使这些人的社会主义经验各不相同，但他们在1970年代至1980年代中期长大成人，毕竟共享着那一时期特定的理解、意义和过程。正如俄国语文学家玛丽娜·克尼亚泽娃所指出的，她所称的“停滞的孩子们”（deti zastoia）这一代人，与前后诸世代不同，没有一个可以围绕之凝聚为队列的“开创性事件”（1990）。较年长诸世代的身份围绕革命、战争、对斯大林的谴责等事件而形成；较年轻世代的身份则围绕苏联的解体而形成。与这些较年长和较年轻的群体不同，最后的苏联世代的共同身份，是由对勃列日涅夫年代那种规范化、无所不在且一成不变的权威话语的共同经验所塑造的。

### Segment 10

**原文**

> This discussion of the last Soviet generation is linked to broader considerations of method employed in the book. This book is not about a representative norm of Soviet life or an average Soviet experience. Rather, it investigates internal shifts that were emerging within the Soviet system during late socialism at the level of discourse, ideology, and knowledge but that became apparent for what they were only much later, when the system collapsed. ### Segment 11

**原文**

> A closely related methodological issue is how the author of this text figures in it. I rarely refer to myself in the text as the “I” of the events, observations, and analysis. This is a conscious decision that I have considered seriously. Being self-reflective about the position of the observer and writer is a crucial ingredient of any analysis, and anthropology has a long-standing tradition of doing this. But this self-reflective position should not be confused with constructing an authorial self that is linguistically present in the text as sovereign and unitary. The authorial voice is always deeply decentered and multivoiced, the point that Bakhtin, one of the inspirations of this book, argued forcefully. This book could only become possible because of the multiple temporal, spatial, and cultural decenterings of my authorial self. The book is written partly through the voice of someone who had a personal experience of living in the Soviet Union during the late socialist period and witnessed the Soviet Union’s disintegration, but equally so through the voice of someone who has lived for the last fifteen years in the United States, who studied in an American graduate school, who become a professional anthropologist in the United States, and who learned to occupy a retrospective position and dif­ erent cultural and linguistic locations to reconsider and analyze the meanings and origins of past events. Furthermore, this book is provoked by experiencing not only Soviet life, but also post-Soviet transformations and Western and postsocialist social science writings about both. **译文**

> 对最后的苏联世代的这番讨论，与本书所采用的方法的更广泛考量相关联。本书所论并非苏联生活的代表性规范，也非平均的苏联经验。毋宁说，它考察的是晚期社会主义时期苏联体制内部在话语、意识形态和知识层面上正在浮现的种种转变——这些转变直到很久以后体制崩溃时，其真实面目才得以显现。正因如此，本分析没有涉及许多重要的历史事件、政治发展、经济状况、社会阶级、族群或性别差异。它聚焦的毋宁是来自俄罗斯不同城镇的年轻一代受过教育的城市居民，他们曾参与苏联国家的意识形态机构、仪式和话语，并从事从科学到文学和音乐的各种文化活动。尽管这一队列的话语、活动、关系和价值观未必代表那个时期平均的社会经验，它们却是一面有力的透镜，透过它，该体制内部正在浮现的转变变得清晰可见。一个密切相关的方法论问题是，本文作者如何在文本中呈现自身。我很少在文本中把自己当作事件、观察和分析的“我”来指称。这是我经过认真考虑之后作出的自觉决定。对观察者和写作者自身位置的自我反思，是任何分析的关键要素，人类学在这方面有着悠久的传统。但这种自我反思的立场，不应与在文本中以语言在场的方式建构一个主权而统一的作者自我混为一谈。作者的声音永远是深度去中心化、多声部的——这是启发本书的思想家之一巴赫金所着力论证的观点。本书之所以可能，恰恰是因为我这个作者自我经历了多重时间、空间和文化上的去中心化。本书部分地是通过这样一个人的声音写成的：他亲身经历过苏联晚期社会主义时期在苏联的生活，目睹了苏联的解体；但同样地，也通过另一个人的声音写成：他在美国生活了十五年，在美国的研究生院学习，在美国成为职业人类学家，并学会占据回顾性的位置和不同的文化与语言场域，去重新思考和解析过往事件的意义与起源。此外，本书的缘起不仅在于对苏联生活的亲历，也在于对后苏联转型的体验，以及对这两者的西方和后社会主义社会科学书写。认识到以下文本之所以可能，全赖于我这位作者的声音所处的多重去中心化位置与时间性，这让我不愿以第一人称写作，也对“本土人类学家”这一标签感到不安。

### Segment 2

**原文**

### Segment 4

**原文**

> Chapter 4 shifts the analysis from the practices and contexts of ideological production to the contexts of cultural milieus4 47 based on networks of friends, common intellectual pursuits, and practices of obshchenie (endless conversations, interactions, and forms of “being with others”).
